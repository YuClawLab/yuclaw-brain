**Institutional Oil Brief**

**Supply/Demand Assessment:**
The EIA reported a build of 2.0M barrels in crude oil inventories for the week ending July 17, bringing total inventory levels to 411.7M barrels. This suggests that supply is outpacing demand, which could put downward pressure on prices.

**Price Outlook for WTI and Brent:**
Given the recent build in inventories and modest price declines (-0.65% for WTI and -0.34% for Brent), we expect prices to remain range-bound in the near term. WTI is likely to oscillate between $80-$85, while Brent will probably trade between $86-$91.

**Energy Equity Positioning:**

* **XOM (ExxonMobil)**: Down 1.12%, XOM's price decline mirrors the broader market weakness. We maintain a neutral stance on XOM.
* **CVX (Chevron)**: With a 1.27% drop, CVX's stock performance is also closely tied to oil prices. We recommend holding CVX for now.
* **COP (ConocoPhillips)**: COP's 1.28% decline suggests investors are cautious about the company's prospects. However, its strong balance sheet and diversified asset base warrant a long-term buy rating.
* **SLB (Schlumberger)**: SLB's underperformance (-3.01%) may present an attractive buying opportunity for those with a high-risk tolerance.

**Risk Factors:**

1. Geopolitical tensions: Escalating conflicts in key oil-producing regions could disrupt supply and send prices soaring.
2. OPEC+ production cuts: Reduced output from major producers might offset the recent inventory build, supporting higher prices.
3. Global economic slowdown: Softening demand due to an economic downturn would further pressure oil prices.

**Specific Trade Recommendation:**
Buy COP (ConocoPhillips) with a target price of $120 and stop-loss at $105. The company's solid fundamentals, attractive valuation, and potential for long-term growth justify this investment decision.
