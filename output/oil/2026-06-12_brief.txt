**Institutional Oil Brief**

**Supply/Demand Assessment:**
The EIA inventory report indicates a draw of 7.2M barrels for the period ending June 5, 2026, resulting in a total inventory of 426.5M barrels. This draw suggests that demand is outpacing supply, which could lead to further upward pressure on prices.

**Price Outlook:**
WTI and Brent prices have both experienced declines, with WTI falling by 1.62% to $84.88 and Brent decreasing by 1.66% to $87.33. However, considering the draw in EIA inventories, we believe this dip is likely a temporary correction. We expect WTI and Brent prices to rebound and trend upwards in the near term, potentially reaching $88-90 for WTI and $91-93 for Brent.

**Energy Equity Positioning:**
The energy sector has seen mixed performance, with ExxonMobil (XOM) rising 0.28% to $147.01, Chevron (CVX) increasing 0.75% to $187.22, ConocoPhillips (COP) surging 1.4% to $116.98, and Schlumberger (SLB) edging up 0.32% to $56.18. The Energy ETF has also gained 0.75% to $57.55. We recommend overweight positions in XOM and CVX, considering their strong upstream portfolios and potential for increased cash flow generation.

**Risk Factors:**
Key risks to our outlook include:

1. Geopolitical tensions affecting global supplies.
2. Potential for demand destruction due to economic downturns or COVID-19 resurgence.
3. Unexpected increases in non-OPEC supply.

**Trade Recommendation:**
Buy August 2026 WTI calls with a strike price of $88, targeting a potential upside of 4-5% to $92-93. This trade recommendation is based on our expectation of a rebound in WTI prices following the EIA inventory draw and the overall bullish sentiment in the energy sector.
