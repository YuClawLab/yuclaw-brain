**Institutional Oil Brief**

**Supply/Demand Assessment:**
The EIA reported a draw of 7.2M barrels for the period ending June 5, 2026, bringing total inventory to 426.5M barrels. This draw indicates a slight tightening in the supply/demand balance. However, the absolute level of inventories remains relatively high. We interpret this data point as neutral to slightly bullish.

**Price Outlook:**
WTI and Brent prices are experiencing mild downward pressure, with WTI down 0.38% and Brent down 0.56%. Despite this short-term weakness, we maintain a constructive view on oil prices due to the ongoing drawdown in inventories. We expect WTI to trade in the $80-$85 range and Brent in the $82-$87 range over the near term.

**Energy Equity Positioning:**
We note that energy equities are underperforming the broader market, with ExxonMobil (XOM), Chevron (CVX), ConocoPhillips (COP), and Schlumberger (SLB) down 4.14%, 3.64%, 4.03%, and 4.4%, respectively. This weakness presents a buying opportunity in our view, particularly for XOM and CVX, which have strong balance sheets and resilient dividend yields.

**Risk Factors:**

* Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East
* Potential for US production growth to surprise to the upside
* Weakening global demand due to economic slowdown
