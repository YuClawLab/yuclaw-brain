**Institutional Oil Brief - 2026-07-07**

**Supply/Demand Assessment:**
The EIA reported a draw of 3.8M barrels for the period ending June 26, bringing total inventory to 408.4M barrels. While this draw is modestly supportive, it only reverses a fraction of the recent builds. The current inventory level still represents a relative surplus compared to historical averages. This data supports our view that crude oil supply remains sufficiently robust to meet demand.

**Price Outlook:**

* **WTI**: We expect WTI prices to trade range-bound ($70-$75) in the near-term, weighed down by bearish fundamentals (elevated inventory levels and increasing shale production). Today's 0.01% price increase does not alter this expectation.
* **Brent**: Despite a slightly more significant price gain (0.03%), we remain cautious about Brent prices due to concerns over slowing global demand growth and increased competition from non-OPEC supply.

**Energy Equity Positioning:**

* **ExxonMobil (XOM)**: Given its relatively strong earnings performance, XOM remains well-positioned. However, with the stock up 3.85% today, we would consider taking profits.
* **Chevron (CVX)**: Despite a 3.52% increase, CVX still appears undervalued relative to peers, driven by solid execution and promising Permian Basin exposure.
* **ConocoPhillips (COP)**: COP's strong equity performance (+4.69%) may be due for a pullback as investor enthusiasm begins to outpace fundamental growth drivers.
* **Schlumberger (SLB)**: We maintain our cautious stance on SLB, which has trailed major producers and service sector peers in recent quarters.

**Risk Factors:**

1. **Macroeconomic uncertainty**: Weakness in key global economies could translate into lower demand for crude oil.
2. **Geopolitical risks**: Escalations in the Middle East or other regions may disrupt production and push prices higher.
3. **Oil industry dislocation**: Sustained low prices might eventually lead to underinvestment in long-term projects.

**Trade Recommendation:**
Buy Chevron (CVX) with a target price of $190 (+9% from current prices), leveraging the company's strategic advantage in the Permian Basin and relatively undervalued stock performance.
