WHY

This card states what the evidence record can and cannot support for UUUU — derived, field by field, from the registered artifacts listed under SOURCES. States such as PENDING, UNKNOWN, NOT_APPLICABLE, NOT_ESTIMABLE and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE are findings in their own right: they say precisely how far the record goes today. Nothing on this card is chosen by hand; re-running the renderer against the same artifacts reproduces it byte for byte.

Research state here never translates to a trading signal, score, or portfolio action — the two vocabularies are structurally separated and gate-enforced.
