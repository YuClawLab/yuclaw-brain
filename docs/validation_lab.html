Verification scope and statistical limits

Exit 0 checks the core expected statistical tables and bundled signal-ledger consistency. External provenance is UNVERIFIED unless a separately acquired ledger and its independently obtained SHA-256 are supplied. Historical in-sample inputs and vendor-derived returns are not independently authenticated. Use --require-external-ledger to require full external coverage of forward inputs.

Forward statistical estimates are exploratory; fixed-sample p-values are not anytime-valid under repeated inspection. Read current uncertainty and power in the statistical tables.

In-sample results are a historical reconstruction with later scoring design and approximated market inputs, not a contemporaneous forward record. The declared Llama 3.1 pretraining cutoff predates the filings; see the canonical look-ahead statement.

Price-return definitions, missing prices and corporate-action exclusions limit interpretation; these results are not a total-return investment performance claim.

IC rows are signal snapshots; repeated issuer/date observations may occur. Snapshot count, distinct issuer/date count and independent information count differ.

The selected universe includes equities and funds with overlapping exposures; it does not represent independent randomly sampled firms.

Insider evidence: batch coverage 2026-02-18 to 2026-05-15; live from 2026-07-16; the gap is backfilled with ingestion-time available_as_of.

Panel 2 · In-Sample Replay — Educational replay only (n=13 rebalances) · collapsed

In-sample look-ahead statement. The in-sample replay rows (signal dates 2026-02-18 to 2026-05-13, evidence window 2026-02-18 to 2026-05-17) were built from evidence events extracted by one language model, Meta Llama 3.1 70B Instruct (served locally as yuclaw-llm-70b), whose published pretraining cutoff is December 2023 (Meta model card). Form 4 events in the window come from a deterministic XML parser with no language model. The earliest in-sample date is about 26 months after that cutoff, so no filing text in the window could have been seen in training: there is no parametric look-ahead from the filings themselves, and the model's general market knowledge also ends before the window begins. The replay engine flags any as-of date before 2024-07-01 as inside the model's training window; no in-sample date triggers it. In-sample results nonetheless remain a replay reconstruction, not a live record: the scoring design was finalized in May 2026, after the window it is replayed over, market-layer components read approximated inputs, and no in-sample signal was exposed to external challenge in real time. In-sample results are therefore treated as systematically optimistic and educational only; the forward record (signal dates from 2026-05-20) is the look-ahead-free record.

Definitions (exact internal rubric, deterministic verifier — no LLM in the loop):
Corpus grounding = points_grounded / points_total across the filing corpus, where an agent key-point is grounded iff it carries ≥1 citation that verifies as a verbatim (whitespace/case-normalized) span of the source filing AND every numeric token in the point appears within those verified quotes; ungrounded points are discarded with the reason recorded.
Citation fidelity = citations_verified / citations_total — the share of quoted spans an agent cites that locate as verbatim spans of the source filing after whitespace/case normalization. Verifier source: v5/swarm/grounding.py.

Methodology summary

Equal-weighted cohorts, rebalanced at each signal date, ranked by composite total_score; top/bottom decile (~10%, currently 8 of 79). References: the equal-weight universe cohort (all scored tickers, identical rebalance schedule) and SPY. Returns are close-to-close from internal price_history (derived statistics only — raw prices never shown). Inclusion rule: a signal date enters the study only if it scored ≥40 tickers; a ticker contributes only when entry and exit closes both exist. The in-sample replay's final holding period is capped at forward Day 0 so the two panels' return windows never overlap. The two panels are never blended. Annualized figures are intentionally omitted — annualizing a weeks-long window is misleading; cumulative return over N trading days is shown instead. Full methodology, including the in-sample look-ahead disclosure, is in methodology/validation_lab.md.
