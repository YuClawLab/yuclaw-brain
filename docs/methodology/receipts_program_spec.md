# Outsider receipt program — prospective specification (v7 candidate, PROPOSAL)

**Status: unadopted proposal.** Nothing here is registered. Adoption requires (1) an owner-issued registration record `{protocol_id, anchor, registered_at, policy_version}` — the window anchor (UTC date) and the actual registration instant (RFC3339 UTC, microseconds) are separate fields; the anchor never precedes the registration instant; a supplied anchor alone is not adoption — (2) a designated reviewer appointment, (3) the applicable privacy review, (4) owner-sent recruitment. Earlier attempts are never backdated into a prospective program: an attempt observed or first imported before `registered_at` (including earlier on the same UTC day) is `pre-registration` forever; a correction cannot move it forward because eligibility uses the earliest observed and imported instants of its whole lineage. The engine that implements this contract exists in `v3/receipts/` and runs today in local/synthetic mode.

## Records (four, kept distinct)
- **Submission** (`receipt-1`): attempt_id, activity_id, opaque participant_id and group_id, relationship ∈ {OWNER-AFFILIATED, RELATED-DISCLOSED, UNRELATED, UNKNOWN}, execution_control ∈ {SELF, ASSISTED, OPERATOR-RUN}, assistance (closed enumeration), incentive_outcome_dependent, outcome ∈ {REPRODUCED, FAILED, INCONCLUSIVE}, observed_at (UTC, µs), artifact_binding {artifact_type, sha256, size_bytes}, release_identity {tag, source_sha} (descriptive only), environment {os, python}, limitations, private. A submission can describe; it cannot assert qualification, verification, approval or authority (such fields are stripped with a diagnostic).
- **Observation**: what the verifier established from actual bytes. FULL binding only when sha256 and byte length match; unavailable bytes stay UNVERIFIED.
- **Review**: a reviewer's decision bound to the exact receipt digest, the policy version and the reviewer's APPOINTMENT (role + credential + policy, each appointment designated or synthetic on its own; a legacy or ambiguous appointment is HELD and reviews nothing). A real qualification is resolved against the immutable appointment record (designated, same role and policy, appointed before the decision) — never against the review line's own label; a legacy review without appointment evidence requires a new explicit review. A later credential replacement or revocation changes current authority only; it never rewrites an earlier review's binding.
- **Public export**: a fresh typed object; pseudonyms are store-keyed; private fields never leave.

## Receipt categories (candidate)
Every submission carries an `activity_type`: **REPLICATION** (reproduce a published artifact; outcome REPRODUCED/FAILED/INCONCLUSIVE), **WITNESS_REVIEW** (a person examined the methodology and left a reviewable receipt; outcome REVIEWED/DECLINED; artifact = the methodology page or bundle examined), **AUDIT_BREAK_ATTEMPT** (an outsider tried to break a published check; outcome NO_BREAK/BREAK_FOUND/INCONCLUSIVE; a found break stays visible; internal test runs are not audits) and **REFUSAL** (a real user asked for an unsupported conclusion and the product refused; outcome REFUSED/NOT_REFUSED; the request text and the user's identity stay private and a refusal receipt never carries a public note; automated check-claim UNSUPPORTED/NOT_IDENTIFIABLE responses are private telemetry, never public refusal receipts; a receipt that fails qualification is a different category from a refusal). Each category is receipted, observed and reviewed under the same rules; displayed counts are derived per category; unreceipted relationships are never counted ("receipted: 0 · unreceipted relationships not counted").

## Exact-release coverage and the target manifest
Program-wide exact-artifact evidence counts every qualified successful reproduction of exact wheel/sdist bytes, any release. Exact-target evidence is measured only against an independently supplied **release target manifest** (`yuclaw-release-target/1`: label REHEARSAL/RC/FINAL, release tag/version/source sha/tree, and the allowed `(artifact_type, sha256, size_bytes)` bindings); only qualified successful attempts whose observed type, hash AND length equal a target artifact count. A FINAL target is generated only from the frozen Phase-2 release record and delivered as separate release evidence (`docs/receipts/target_manifest.json`, GitHub release asset); it is never embedded in the artifacts it describes. Without a target manifest the coverage is UNBOUND, not zero. A wheel never covers its sdist; an unchanged RC artifact covers the identical final artifact.

## Counting rules
- Primary population = **qualified attempts** (validated + FULL binding + designated QUALIFIED review + eligible relationship/control/incentive); qualified FAILED and INCONCLUSIVE are retained.
- Successful cohort = qualified AND REPRODUCED, reported separately; its artifact set is labeled as such.
- Distinct persons and control groups are deduplicated across activities and versions inside a registered window (candidate: fixed 28-day UTC windows from the anchor; day 27 is window 0, day 28 is window 1). Eligibility is a typed value (prospective / pre_registration / pre_anchor / other_protocol / unwindowed) applied to the whole lineage of an attempt: the earliest observed and first-imported instants must be at or after the registration instant and the earliest observed date on or after the anchor, and the counting window is pinned to that earliest observed date — a reviewed correction stays in its original window, is rendered CORRECTED with its supersession date, and never re-enters a later window as new accrual; a new independent attempt is a distinct receipt. Comparing two snapshots that differ only by an eligibility-preserving supersession shows zero additions, zero window movement and one disposition change. Records of another protocol are never reassigned: they stay visible in their own unregistered bucket. Without registration: unwindowed, "registration pending"; window zero is never fabricated.
- Package evidence (wheel, sdist) is separate from site/endpoint/chain checks. A receipt covers only the exact bytes it binds: an RC wheel receipt does not cover the sdist or a rebuilt final wheel.
- Program evidence (any release; legacy PREFIX_ONLY log) is separate from exact-release evidence.

## Proposed floor (historical, unadopted)
Three successful qualified non-owner reproductions spanning two independent control groups and two supported environments, plus one scoped external challenge and one historical-consistency check. **Adoption status: none on record.** The floor does not gate any release until adopted; it is not waived by a date.

## Public identity
The public receipt id is a store-keyed digest of the private receipt digest (`r-…`). The private digest commits to participant ids and private fields and is never published, so no public commitment can be tested against guessable private data. Pseudonyms are store-keyed HMACs. Free text is published only when the private denylist and the language rail are both available and pass; a missing denylist is not an empty denylist (nothing is published).

## Challenges
A challenge states a **criterion**: a machine-checkable one with a single allowlisted verifier (`packet-integrity-and-replay` → offline packet verification; `artifact-reproduction-by-qualified-receipt` → a qualified successful receipt binding exactly the revised artifact) or `general` (resolved only by a separately authenticated reviewer evaluation of the stated criterion). A verifier that cannot test the criterion records NOT_EVALUATED and the challenge stays unresolved; a claim id copied into a record is not an evaluated assertion.
CONFIRMED, REFUTED and RESOLVED dispositions of a real challenge require a DESIGNATED appointment; synthetic appointments dispose synthetic challenges only; HELD authority disposes nothing. RESOLVED requires the revised artifact's actual bytes and a verification record that the store itself produced by executing the permitted deterministic verifier (offline packet verification binding the revised artifact to an observed packet artifact, or a qualified successful receipt binding the revised artifact); a submitted label or a bare digest never resolves a challenge.

## Privacy
Identity mappings, relationship supporting facts, contacts and reviewer notes stay in the private store. No public hash commitment to guessable private material. Free text is published only with permission and after the language rail and the private denylist sweep.
