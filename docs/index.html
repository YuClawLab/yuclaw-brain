YUCLAW v4.0

Evidence-First Financial Research

YUCLAW reads SEC filings and turns them into research classifications — never buy/sell calls. Every signal links to its source filings, carries an Evidence Quality grade, and is hash-anchored in a public, git-anchored ledger so anyone can independently verify it.

YUCLAW research output. Not investment advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Signal labels are research classifications, not buy/sell recommendations.
