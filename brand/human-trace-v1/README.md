# YuClaw — The Human Trace

**Status: approved by the user for YuClaw. Use the selected assets as delivered, with no further design changes.**

The logo expresses the founder's meaning: humanity taking tiny steps through the universe, leaving intermittent traces that quietly show we were here.

## The symbol

Three separated, tapered impressions form an asymmetric Y. The gaps express intermittent presence. The ascending movement suggests continuing exploration. The surrounding empty space evokes the vastness of the universe. This is the intended interpretation of the mark, not a claim that every viewer will read it identically.

The lettering is exactly **YuClaw**. All authored material is English.

## Design direction

Use the dark master when introducing the cosmic meaning; use the light master on research documents and other light surfaces. Preserve generous clear space and the open gaps between the three parts. Do not add stars, orbital rings, market arrows, or claw details to this concept.

The idea of fading is expressed in the static mark through interruption. No animation or further visual changes are planned under this approval.

Use the approved PNG assets directly for v8 integration. Preserve their geometry, lettering, proportions, and colors. Placement may use proportional display sizing and clear space; do not redraw, restyle, animate, or replace the files under this approval. The digests in the asset manifest identify the approved originals.
